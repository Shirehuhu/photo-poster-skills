# 场景提炼 · Scene Distillation Zine v1.3

作者 / Author: Zeejay0

Modified edition by Shirehuhu (2026-10-14; shared 2026-10-15). Original copyright, source and attribution: see NOTICE. Governed by the unchanged upstream Personal Non-Commercial License in LICENSE. This is not an official upstream release.

Use the photograph as semantic evidence, never a layer in the final art. The work should stand alone while retaining a subtle, specific, credible source connection. This is authored illustration, not filtering, tracing or rotoscoping.

## Source and expressive brief

Inspect the photo; identify its semantic nucleus, 1–2 inseparable core subjects, 1–3 supporting cues, dominant gesture, one meaningful spatial relationship, visual weight, palette/material/weather, emotional residue, discard list and transformation opportunities. Preserve 2–4 source anchors, not the photographic framing.

Build one chain: source fact → emotional residue → expressive proposition → central tension → source-derived visual metaphor → visible formal embodiment → interpretive opening.

Use one central metaphor; supporting forms must not introduce unrelated themes or universal-symbol clichés.

Choose one primary tension, optionally one subordinate: intimacy/distance, shelter/confinement, movement/stillness, smallness/vastness, warmth/coldness, memory/disappearance, order/growth, visibility/concealment or permanence/fragility. Generic “quiet/healing/dreamy” labels are insufficient without a visible reason.

Make scale/space, direction/rhythm, boundary, color, material and typography perform the proposition. Resolve the emotional direction while leaving one meaningful relationship open through omission, obstruction, incomplete action, scale shift or text–image gap—not random obscurity.

## Authorial transformation

Recompose, change scale/proportion/spacing/orientation, merge/split/repeat/displace forms, exaggerate gesture/distance, or abstract light/wind/water/shadow into marks. Source-consistent supporting inventions must extend emotion, clarify relationships, establish rhythm, balance weight, guide the eye or strengthen the metaphor. Remove additions whose only purpose is decoration.

Editorial abstraction normally removes about 65–90% of descriptive detail. Retain the semantic nucleus, dominant gesture and one specific cue. Facial identity may be generalized unless likeness is requested; deliberate simplification must not become accidental malformed anatomy. When likeness, a complete subject or exact text is requested, protect it within the newly illustrated language.

Choose one primary grammar and at most one support: cut-paper mass, dry-print silhouette, broken contour, compressed rhythm, 2–3 fragment stack, or orbit/drift along a source-derived path. Avoid literal full-scene copying, realistic shading, polished vector characters and cute cartoon/anime/children's-book treatment unless requested.

## Composition and edges

Preserve source orientation by default: 3:5 portrait, 5:3 landscape, 3:5 for square/ambiguous input. User ratios override. Recompose inside it; do not stretch, copy framing or enlarge the cluster simply to fill the format.

Start with 68–85% quiet paper, about 12–32% active cluster, one dominant mass, 1–3 supporting forms and one restrained texture field; adjust by actual visual weight and protected subject needs.

Choose by source geometry: asymmetric island, torn window, directional drift, rhythmic circulation, staggered fragments, vertical tension, or auxiliary constellation. For a constellation, disperse a genuine source-derived supporting motif with unequal scale/spacing/density; do not invent confetti or a decorative border. Keep figure–ground clarity, optical balance, hierarchy, proximity/continuation, breathing room and a deliberate entry→focal encounter→movement→quiet exit.

Choose one primary transition and at most one subordinate:
- irregular torn fibers;
- 2–3 narrow irregular grayscale bands for shallow separation;
- source-directed sparse stipple/halftone dissolution;
- 1–3 small source-derived neutral edge gestures;
- natural isolated contour, with no added tear/rim/stipple/halo/border.

Use internal paper gaps and at most 0–2 useful crossing forms. Flat scan, shallow material separation, no cast shadow, curled paper, bevel, floating fragments or heavy 3D. An isolated contour is newly illustrated, never photographic clipping.

## Color modes

Resolve hue, value, chroma, material, adjacency/area and role through source resonance, analogous harmony, temperature counterpoint or focused complement. Choose an exact high-chroma print hue appropriate to the source—not always blue, not an example chosen by habit.

### Standard Accent Mode (default)

Keep paper/most ink subdued; give one unmistakable high-chroma hue the role of focal entry, counterweight, bridge, direction or rhythm. Use about 0.8–3% of the poster or 10–30% of the active cluster. One main accent may have 1–2 echoes; all echoes together stay below 25% of accent area. State the eye path and physical color carrier. Do not mute/pastel the accent unless requested.

A distributed supporting accent is allowed only for a meaningful repeatable source motif. Use unequal instances and open intervals, all in the same exact hue and within the same area allowance. It replaces the main-accent/echo scheme; it does not add a second color system.

### 单色块模式 (exact trigger)

Activate only when the request contains the exact text 单色块模式. Do not infer it merely from minimalism.

Use paper tone, one neutral ink system and exactly one contiguous fully saturated pictorial field. All other illustrated objects/outlines/textures use charcoal, graphite, warm gray, brown-black or off-black; no chromatic supporting tints or accent echoes. The field is solid/opaque with subtle paper/ink defects, about 3–12% of the canvas or 25–65% of the active cluster. Derive its shape from a source form, aperture, silhouette, space or semantic nucleus; it is the focal entry/spatial idea, not a detached swatch.

Typography may use neutral ink and/or that same saturated hue without introducing a new hue. This text exception does not permit extra colored pictorial objects or split fields. Explicitly state the one connected field and neutral remainder in the prompt.

## Authorial typography

Language, amount, family, consistency, alignment, direction, scale, completion, placement, image interaction and intentional legibility are expressive choices, not a mandatory neat caption. Chinese, English, bilingual text, authored fragments/marks, repetition, long passages or no text are available. Text can be a countervoice, interruption, rhythm, image-form or primary subject, and may cross/rotate/crop/overlap/obscure forms when meaningful.

Every text decision must deepen proposition, tension, metaphor or interpretive opening. Distinguish intentional abstraction/occlusion from accidental garbling: requested readable or exact wording must remain correct; do not invent quotations or attribution as factual claims.

## Generation and acceptance

Use the actual supplied image as semantic reference with the built-in image capability; consult imagegen for tool mechanics. Send only necessary prompt/reference; do not archive private sources or upload them elsewhere.

Compile five compact sections: expression/visible consequence; canvas/attention geometry; distilled subject/creative rewrite; edge/color mode/text; print mood and relevant exclusions. Explicitly require original illustration, paper and typography only: no reproduced, embedded, cropped, collaged, traced or retained photographic pixels or photorealistic regions.

Inspect the actual raster before delivery, including thumbnail hierarchy, source anchors, visible proposition/tension/metaphor, meaningful opening, quiet-space balance, grammar/edge discipline and the selected color-mode rules. Check requested likeness/anatomy and exact text when applicable. If a central requirement fails, make a targeted correction without repainting successful regions; after two failed correction attempts explain the limit rather than presenting it as passed.

Reject photo windows/fragments, tracing, arbitrary symbols/scattering, evenly repeated motifs, sticker outlines/halos, tape/stamps, dense scrapbooking, competing bright hues, unrequested logos/CTA/watermarks, commercial/mockup/3D/neon/cinematic-lighting drift. Solid mode also rejects tinted supporting forms, detached swatches and split pictorial fields.

Export at least 2K long edge in the selected ratio without stretching; disclose non-generative enlargement. Preserve source/prior files and original basenames using separate output folders. Return the raster/path and a brief Chinese creative idea; detailed art-direction notes or the final prompt only when requested. Do not append promotions or generation-count notices.
