# 纸浆浮雕海报 · 风格 6

One independent 3:4 portrait poster per source. Selecting 6 explicitly means no original/result comparison, top/bottom split, grid or rectangular photo frame. Do not repeat the routine collage question or inherit an older batch's yes. A later explicit composite request changes this preset: confirm content/layout/ratio/text and keep the standalone poster.

## Subject and space

Inspect the local source with view_image. Naturally resolve color before material treatment, preferably in one pass. Preserve recognizable structure, pose, proportions, core colors, perspective and essential spatial relationships while deleting distracting background.

Use asymmetric composition with the subject mainly middle-to-lower right and about 60% clean warm ivory art-paper negative space. This is an optical target, not a crop quota: reduce/reposition the complete subject before cutting hands, clothing, props or architecture. For people preserve identity, expression, anatomy/hands and defining details; add no people or fabricated missing anatomy.

## Delicate relief

Keep the subject's realistic interior clear. Toward its perimeter, gradually blend into irregular paper fibers, localized mineral-pigment impasto, dry-brushed exposed paper and torn edges dissolving into the sheet.

Keep texture close to the subject, with varied density and broken rhythms—not a uniform border, photo mount or background-wide effect. Soft side light and minute shadows describe thin layered relief: fine, matte, light and airy, never plastic or heavy modeled 3D. Empty paper stays clean, not yellowed, stained or distressed.

## Chinese editorial type

Author a short scene-grounded Chinese title in upper-left breathing room, small slender Songti/modern Mincho; two lines are allowed. Do not squeeze/deform glyphs to make them tall.

Add a still-smaller English phrase, discreet edition-style number and one short rule sampled from the source palette. The number is a design index, not a factual date/location. Use size/weight contrast, strict alignment, generous spacing/leading and only minimal side/footer microcopy echoing the subject's contour.

All text is exact, readable, restrained and subordinate. Invent no biography, location, date, award or factual caption. Resolve Chinese glyph errors rather than hiding gibberish as texture; if the tool cannot render small text correctly, report the limit.

## Production and acceptance

Use the built-in image editing capability with the actual photo; consult imagegen for tool mechanics. Send only needed references/prompt; this preset does not authorize editing unrelated photos or publishing private material.

Brief source invariants, exact 3:4 standalone layout, subject/blank-space balance, localized material transition and exact copy. Prefer one focused pass; inspect the actual result, subject/anatomy/edges, clear interior, shallow shadows, negative space and every text line. Apply localized corrections only; after two failed correction attempts explain the limit, not a false pass.

Export exact 3:4 at 2K or above (1536×2048 or 1920×2560). Never stretch; disclose non-generative enlargement instead of claiming restored native detail. Preserve originals/prior outputs and basenames in separate result folders; no unsolicited processing/version suffixes.

Reject comparisons/grids/split panels/photo frames, giant bold titles, dense copy, garbled text, watermarks, extra people, deformed subjects, plastic shine, heavy modeled relief, yellow stains and full-background texture.
