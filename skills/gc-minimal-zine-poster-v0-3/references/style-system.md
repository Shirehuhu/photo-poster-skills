# Minimal Zine Editorial Style System

A flat scanned paper field carries one small visual event: micro-collage, specimen, type-object, faded photographic fragment or conceptual relation. Collage is one grammar, not a mandatory template.

## Fixed identity

- Default portrait 3:5; user ratios override. Full-frame warm matte paper with fibers/fine grain, restrained dust/aging/scan noise; no border or mockup.
- About 70–90% open paper, one main cluster about 8–25%. Preserve an edit target's declared complete subject before forcing these ranges.
- One central metaphor/relation, not a full illustrated scene or inventory.
- Flat orthographic scan, diffuse light, low-to-medium contrast; no hard shadow or mockup depth.
- Sparse typewriter/old or fine serif/mono/restrained small sans. Type reads as note, fragment, private sentence or source-supported label, not advertising hierarchy.
- Paper and subdued support inks carry one clearly saturated main accent visible at thumbnail scale.
- Focal element and type share a printed/scanned material world: selective halftone, xerox softness, risograph grain, letterpress bleed, scanline, paper edge or slight misregistration.
- Quiet, poetic, distant, archival/diary/memory-like indie-zine character.

## Color

Choose one main high-chroma hue appropriate to the content: cobalt/ultramarine, cyan/violet, magenta, lemon, pear green, orange, tomato red or another justified hue. Main area: about 0.8–2.5% of canvas or 15–35% of cluster.

Carry color through the subject, cutout, block, photo region or bold fragmented type; do not routinely reduce it to a registration dot. A secondary hue is optional, subordinate and at most about 10% of total chromatic area.

Low-contrast/muted wording applies to paper, photographic support and secondary ink, not the main accent. Do not desaturate an edit target's protected recognizable colors merely to enforce an accent rule. Pale/pastel/near-monochrome main accents require a user choice.

## Variables

- Paper: warm white/ivory, light gray, old-paper yellow, pale khaki or light kraft.
- Cluster: high/low center, left/right middle, lower-left/upper-right third or deliberate offset.
- Focal form: faded photo, clipping, flat silhouette, opaque ink block, printed illustration, specimen, translucent overlay, texture window or type-form.
- Structure: single event, dual small panels, overlap, irregular cutout, orbiting marks or type-led.
- Type: edge phrase, source-supported archive microtext, diagonal fragments, scattered letters, block text, ghost text or nearly textless.
- Marks: restrained fine/dashed line, arrow, transparent rectangle, dots, registration or hand curve; optional, never default filler.
- Mood follows the content rather than an automatic subject menu.

## Subject and exclusions

Derive the subject/metaphor from the content before choosing its material. For an abstract theme, one near-touching pair, interrupted line, aperture, missing repeat, changed-state pair or displaced everyday object may carry meaning; examples are not fixed symbols.

Avoid full-bleed scenes, ad/product/logo/CTA systems, glossy/mockup/UI-white aesthetics, cinematic lighting, hard shadows, 3D/neon/cyberpunk, cute cartoon, fashion drama, busy scrapbooks, multicolor chaos and long clean text blocks. For reference-only images do not reuse sample words, brands, signatures, watermark, exact dates/locations, identity or layout. Edit-target preservation and exact user-supplied wording remain separate contracts.
